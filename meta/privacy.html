隱私政策 / Privacy Policy

本頁適用於應用程式「my-assistant」（下稱本應用程式）。本應用程式為帳號擁有者個人使用之社群帳號管理工具，僅用於管理其本人之 Instagram 帳號與 Facebook 粉絲專頁。

This page applies to the application “my-assistant” (the “App”). The App is a personal social-media management tool used solely by the account owner to manage their own Instagram account and Facebook Page.

1. 我們取得哪些資料 / Data We Access

本應用程式透過 Meta 官方 API 存取：帳號擁有者自己發佈的內容（貼文、影片、限時動態）、粉絲專頁與 Instagram 帳號的公開洞察數據（觸及、讚數、留言數）、以及發佈在其內容上的留言與訊息，目的僅為協助帳號擁有者管理與回覆。

Via official Meta APIs, the App accesses: content published by the account owner, public insights (reach, likes, comment counts), and comments/messages on the owner's own content, solely to help the owner manage and respond.

2. 我們如何使用資料 / How Data Is Used

資料僅用於帳號擁有者本人之內容管理、留言回覆與成效分析。本應用程式不對第三方販售、出租或分享任何資料，亦不用於廣告投放或行為追蹤。

Data is used only for the owner's own content management, replies, and analytics. No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties, nor used for advertising or tracking.

3. 資料儲存與保留 / Storage & Retention

存取憑證儲存於帳號擁有者本人之私人裝置，未上傳至任何第三方伺服器。留言與數據僅於處理當下讀取，不建立長期資料庫。

Access credentials are stored on the owner's private device only and are never uploaded to third-party servers. Comments and metrics are read transiently and not stored in any long-term database.

4. 資料刪除 / Data Deletion

如欲要求刪除與您相關之任何資料，請透過下方電子郵件聯繫，我們將於 30 日內處理。您亦可隨時於 Instagram「應用程式和網站」設定中移除本應用程式之授權。

To request deletion of any data related to you, contact the email below; requests are handled within 30 days. You may also revoke the App's access at any time via Instagram's “Apps and Websites” settings.
